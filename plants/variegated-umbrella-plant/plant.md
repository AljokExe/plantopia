# Variegated Umbrella Plant

- **Notion page:** https://app.notion.com/p/3f7de382842681a4a357d4f004148981
- **Scientific name:** Schefflera (Heptapleurum) arboricola, variegated form such as 'Gold Capella'
- **Ownership:** Currently Owned
- **Acquired:** unknown

## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | NC State: indoors "filtered light"; "bright, direct light will burn the leaves" |
| Temperature Requirement | 15℃, 30℃ | NC State: not below 60°F (15℃). RHS H1C: 5–10℃ survival. Maximum unverified |
| Humidity Requirement | 50% | Weak: NC State "likes high humidity", no figure |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | NC State: "Allow the soil to dry out and then thoroughly soak it" |
| Special Care Instructions | Let the soil dry out, then soak it thoroughly: soil that stays too wet or too dry makes it drop leaves. Check the leaves for spider mites and scale. | NC State: "If the soil is too wet or too dry the leave will drop off" |

**Botanical note:** Araliaceae. Seriously toxic to dogs and cats (calcium oxalate); sap can irritate skin (NC State).

## Setup

- **Location:** On the floor in a corner, in a green glazed pot.
