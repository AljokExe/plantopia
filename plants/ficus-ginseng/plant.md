## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-10.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade, Full Sun | NC State: "Full sun (6 or more hours)". RHS: "full or filtered light". UF/IFAS: "sensitive to radical changes in light intensities" |
| Temperature Requirement | 18℃, 30℃ | UF/IFAS: interior "65 to 80°F" (18–27℃). NC State: "above 68°F" (20℃). 30℃ is the closest existing option above 27℃ |
| Humidity Requirement | 50%, 80% | NC State: "High humidity (70% - 100%) is preferable". Shop care sheets: 50–60% (weak) |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage | NC State: "Good Drainage". UF/IFAS: media with "good container capacity and aeration" |
| Special Care Instructions | Keep it in one place, out of cold draughts. A move or a sudden change in light or temperature makes it drop leaves. Keep the soil moist, not wet, and water less in winter. | RHS: "resents being moved… can respond by dropping its leaves", "keep just moist over winter". Missouri BG: "Cold drafts will also frequently cause this". UF/IFAS: "Media should be kept moist" |

**Botanical note:** the thick "trunk" is swollen roots ("bulbous, thick roots reminiscent of ginseng that rise out of the ground"), and the top is "sometimes grafted with branches of a smaller-leaved cultivar" (NC State).

**Safety:** RHS lists the sap as a skin allergen. Wear gloves to prune or repot.

## Setup

- **Pot:** green plastic nursery pot inside a grey glazed cachepot (since 2026-10-10). Empty the cachepot after each watering: Illinois Extension says the liner must "never stand in water".
- **Soil:** shop soil, dark and peat-like (from the photo). Plan: repot in spring 2027 into a gritty, well-aerated mix (Penn State: repot "in the spring"; RHS: loam-based compost "with added bark chippings").
- **Location:** desk next to a window (photo 2026-10-10).
