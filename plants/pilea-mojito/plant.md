## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | RHS: partial shade, "Shade from hot sun" |
| Temperature Requirement | 10℃, 23℃ | RHS: H1C for the cultivar, above 10℃ for the species. Maximum unverified |
| Humidity Requirement | Humidity tolerant | NC State (species): tolerant of average home humidity |
