- **Scientific name:** Ficus benjamina, cultivar unconfirmed ('Starlight' or 'Variegata')

## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | NC State: "performs best in bright indirect sunny locations", "very sensitive to light" |
| Temperature Requirement | 18℃, 30℃ | UF/IFAS: interior "65 to 80°F" (18–27℃), keep above 60°F (16℃). 30℃ is the closest existing option above 27℃ |
| Humidity Requirement | 50% | Weak: UF/IFAS says low humidity causes dry, shrivelled leaves, but gives no indoor number |
| Soil Type | Allow soil to dry between waterings | Missouri BG: water when "the top 2 or 3 inches of the soil ball feels dry". NC State: never let it dry out fully |
| Special Care Instructions | Choose a bright spot away from draughts and radiators, then don't move her: a move, or a change in light or temperature, makes her drop leaves. Water when the top 5 cm of soil is dry. | NC State: "if the plant is moved, it may drop leaves", "kept away from drafts". Missouri BG: "Abrupt changes in the light environment… will often trigger significant leaf drop" |

**Safety:** NC State lists it as a problem for cats, dogs and horses.

**Pests:** NC State: "Watch for scale, aphids, mealybugs, thrips, and spider mites."

## Setup

- **Soil:** unknown (shop soil).
- **Location:** corner next to an interior glass wall. She dropped only a few leaves in her first month, so she stays there.
