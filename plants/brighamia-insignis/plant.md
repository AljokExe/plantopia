# Brighamia insignis

- **Notion page:** https://app.notion.com/p/3f5de382842681929e96dd7524536799
- **Scientific name:** Brighamia insignis A. Gray (Campanulaceae); Hawaiian name ʻālula
- **Ownership:** Currently Owned
- **Acquired:** unknown (repotted 2026-10-10)

## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-10. NTBG (National Tropical Botanical Garden, which leads the conservation work on this species) is the only source used for the Notion values.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade, Full Sun | NTBG care guide: "partial shade to full sun". ukhouseplants (shop, weak) disagrees for indoors: "Avoid more than an hour of direct sunlight per day" |
| Temperature Requirement | empty | NTBG gives no range. Weak only: ukhouseplants "above 15℃ (59℉)"; US plant patent PP15433 "tolerate temperatures from 12 to 35° C" (greenhouse observation) |
| Humidity Requirement | empty | NTBG gives no value. Sources disagree: NTBG database "dry coastal grasslands or shrublands"; ukhouseplants (weak) "humid setting" |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | NTBG care guide: "Good drainage and soil aeration are essential; water when potting medium has dried" |
| Special Care Instructions | Let the potting mix dry before you water, and use a free-draining pot. Look under the leaves for spider mites, and keep slugs and snails away. | NTBG care guide: "Spider mites are also a very serious pest"; "Slugs and snails can devour leaves and the tips of all the stems in no time, killing the plant" |

**Botanical note:** "Family: CAMPANULACEAE", "Species Author: A. Gray", "Vernacular: Alula, Haha". It "grows on rocky ledges with little soil or steep sea cliffs" (NTBG database), "native to the island of Kaua'i" (NTBG care guide).

## Setup

- **Pot:** vase with no drainage holes (2026-10-10). To do: drill a hole, or double pot (a plastic pot with holes inside the vase, and empty the vase after each watering). Illinois Extension: "One way is to drill one"; "Slipping a container inside a slightly larger one is called double potting." Do not add gravel under the soil: it "does little to keep soil above it from being saturated by overwatering."
- **Soil:** repotted 2026-10-10. Firm the mix only gently. Penn State: "gently firm the top layer", and the mix "should not be so compacted that it restricts water and air movement into and through the pot."
