## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Full Sun, Partial Shade | RHS: "full sun to partial shade". NC State: bright indirect light indoors |
| Temperature Requirement | 10℃, 30℃ | RHS H2 (1–5℃ minimum). NC State: keep above 45°F (7℃), prefers 65–85°F (18–29℃). 10℃ chosen as a safe minimum |
| Humidity Requirement | Ventilation | NC State: "Maintain good air circulation to avoid fungal diseases" |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | NC State: well-drained, sandy or loamy soil; "water this plant sparingly" |
| Special Care Instructions | Wear gloves: the milky sap badly irritates skin and eyes. Water sparingly and let the soil dry between waterings. | NC State: sap causes "skin irritation, rash, blisters, and severe eye irritation" |

**Botanical note:** Euphorbiaceae. Toxic: RHS "IRRITANT to skin/eye; harmful if eaten". Keep away from pets and children.
