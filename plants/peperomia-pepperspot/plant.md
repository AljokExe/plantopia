# Peperomia 'Pepperspot'

- **Notion page:** https://app.notion.com/p/3f7de382842681f98ebff32fe27f2156
- **Scientific name:** Peperomia 'Pepperspot' (string of coins); parent species uncertain, P. prostrata or P. rotundifolia
- **Ownership:** Currently Owned
- **Acquired:** unknown

## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | Weak: shop guides, bright indirect light, no direct sun; colour is best in good light |
| Temperature Requirement | 15℃, 23℃ | Weak: shop guides give 18–24℃ and warn below 15℃; Waitrose: above 10℃ in winter |
| Humidity Requirement | Humidity tolerant | Weak: shop guides, average indoor humidity is fine |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | Clemson (genus): "Peperomia prefers to dry out between waterings" |
| Special Care Instructions | Bright indirect light keeps the stems red. Water only when the top of the soil is dry: the fine roots rot easily. The stems are fragile, so handle gently; broken pieces root in soil. | Weak: shop guides |

**Botanical note:** Piperaceae. Probably safe for pets: Clemson says most peperomias are; no source covers this cultivar.

## Setup

- **Location:** Hanging in a yellow pot in a macramé hanger at the balcony window.
