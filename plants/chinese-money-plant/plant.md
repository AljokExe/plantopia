## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | RHS: "bright but indirect light". NC State: "Leggy growth results from inadequate light" |
| Temperature Requirement | 10℃, 23℃ | RHS: "always above 10°C". Maximum unverified |
| Humidity Requirement | Humidity tolerant | NC State: tolerant of average home humidity |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | RHS: water once the top few cm dry. NC State: root rot if overwatered |
| Special Care Instructions | Leggy from too little light. In spring, cut the top off and root it; the stem left behind usually sprouts again. Then give it more light and turn the pot often. | RHS: tall stems "can be cut back in spring or summer"; stem-tip cuttings root |

**Botanical note:** Urticaceae. Safe for pets and children (NC State).
