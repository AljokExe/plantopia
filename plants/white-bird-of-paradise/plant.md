# White Bird of Paradise

- **Notion page:** https://app.notion.com/p/3f7de382842681abbd28ff32fe8a5523
- **Scientific name:** Strelitzia nicolai (unconfirmed: S. reginae stays under about 1.5 m and has orange flowers)
- **Ownership:** Currently Owned
- **Acquired:** unknown

## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Full Sun, Partial Shade | RHS: indoors "bright light but shade from hot sun". NC State: "prefers bright, indirect light" |
| Temperature Requirement | 10℃, 30℃ | NC State: no lower than 50°F (10℃); prefers 65–85°F (18–29℃). RHS H1B |
| Humidity Requirement | 50% | Weak: NC State "prefers high humidity", no figure |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Slightly Acid | NC State: "moist, well-drained soils", acidic to neutral pH; water when the top layer is dry |
| Special Care Instructions | Give it the brightest spot you have. Water when the top layer of soil is dry, and sparingly in winter. Repot only about every 2 years: it flowers better slightly pot-bound. | RHS: "Water sparingly in winter" |

**Botanical note:** Strelitziaceae. Mildly toxic to people, dogs and cats (NC State: "Poison Severity: Low").

## Setup

- **Location:** In the bathroom corner by the frosted window, in a large grey self-watering pot.
