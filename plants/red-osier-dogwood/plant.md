**Botanical note:** a suckering, deciduous shrub, 1.5–2.5 m tall (RHS). The red winter stems are brightest on young growth (NC State).

**Watering:** no source gives a schedule. Check the soil and water when the top 2–3 cm is dry: about weekly in autumn, rarely in winter (never when frozen), often in summer.

## Setup

- **Pot:** terracotta, outdoors. Terracotta can crack in frost: move it against the wall or wrap it in hard frost.
- **Soil:** unknown (shop soil).
- **Location:** balcony facing south-east, Amsterdam.
