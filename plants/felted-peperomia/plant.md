## Care (mirrors the Notion properties)

Checked on 2026-10-12.

| Property | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Requirement | Partial Shade | Fairchild: "prefers shade to partial sun". Clemson: direct sun may scorch the leaves |
| Temperature Requirement | 15℃, 30℃ | Clemson (genus): nights "consistently 60°F or higher". Maximum from a shop care sheet (weak) |
| Humidity Requirement | Humidity tolerant | Weak: shop care sheet, average indoor humidity |
| Soil Type | Excellent Drainage, Allow soil to dry between waterings | Fairchild: "well draining soil", "allowed to dry out between waterings" |
| Special Care Instructions | Treat it like a succulent: use a gritty, fast-draining mix and let it dry well between waterings, especially in winter. | Clemson: "Peperomia prefers to dry out between waterings" |

**Botanical note:** Piperaceae. Probably safe for pets: Clemson says most peperomias are, but no source covers this species.

## Setup

- **Location:** On the bedroom dresser in a dark glazed pot with a saucer.
